# GTM Intelligence Engine

## The Idea

Sales reps are only as good as the 
intelligence they have access to.

Most sales tools give them internal signals —
form fills, email opens, website visits.

This engine gives them external signals —
tariffs, geopolitical events, commodity shocks,
supply chain disruptions.

The difference between a rep who reacts
and a rep who anticipates — is intelligence.

---

## What It Does

Monitors the external world continuously.
When a signal is detected and confirmed by 
3 credible sources — it matches that signal 
to the right prospect in the pipeline.

The rep gets alerted before the prospect 
knows they have a problem.

**Engine does the intelligence. 
Human does the persuasion.**

---

## Built For

B2B SaaS sales teams selling into manufacturing.
Proof of concept: LevaData — AI-powered supply 
chain sourcing platform.

---

## What The Rep Gets

- Personalized email draft
- Call talking point  
- Relevant insight to share

Matched to pipeline stage. Ready to use.

---
